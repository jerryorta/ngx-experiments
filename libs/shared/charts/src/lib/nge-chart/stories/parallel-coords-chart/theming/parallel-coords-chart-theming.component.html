Density: the knob that decides what the chart says

line.opacity and line.width are not cosmetics here. Thin and translucent, overlapping records accumulate and the bunching becomes the reading; thick and opaque, each record reads individually and the mass turns into a solid block. Pick the end that matches the record count.

Dense — opacity 0.35, width 1
Sparse — opacity 1, width 3

Hover Contrast

line.dimmedOpacity sets how far the other records recede while one is traced. Hover either chart: too low and the surrounding context is lost, too high and the traced line does not stand out.

Strong dim — 0.04
Soft dim — 0.35

Brush Chrome

The brush slice styles the per-axis selection window and its two edge grips. Both charts hold the same extents — a Weight range and two Origin categories — set through brushExtents alone, which is enough to draw the chrome; onBrush only arms the drag. Keep fillOpacity low enough that the axis, its ticks and the records still read through the window: a brush that hides what it selects defeats the filter it is driving.

Default — primary at 0.18, 18px wide
Emphasised — 0.35, 28px wide, 2px edge
